Hi there! I'm Sasan.

Welcome to my portfolio! I'm a junior studying Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at UC Berkeley. My interests include analog and mixed signal IC design, board level design, semiconductor manufacturing, and circuit design optimiztion. See some of my projects, past and present, below.

Project Highlights

Magnetic Fill Level Sensing

Modular system that contactlessly tracks a float using Hall effect sensors to monitor rocket propellant fill level.

Autonomous Boat Motherboard

PCB managing power regulation and monitoring, data logging, and sensor inputs of an autonomous oceanic drone.

Two-Stage Display Driver Op-Amp

Operational amplifier designed to drive a 272 x 340 60 Hz display with minimal power consumption, validated in Cadence.

Lithography on Thin Films and Oxides

Explored how optimal exposure settings change on various metal thin films and silicon dioxide thicknesses.

Bandpass Treble Boost Guitar Circuit

Filter that attenuates audibly pleasant frequencies (300 Hz - 15 kHz) and attenuates lower and hgher frequencies.

Photosensitive Alarm Clock

PCB that drives a motor in response to changes in light intensity, meant to open window blinds at dawn.

Rocketry Team Electrical Onboarding

Custom-made curriculum guiding new members through the schematic capture and layout design process.
